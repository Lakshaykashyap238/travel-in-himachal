# What the owner sets up

## Done by the owner in /admin (no developer needed)
Everything on the website is content the owner manages, then publishes with **Publish changes** (live in about 3 minutes):

- **Business details:** name, tagline, phone, WhatsApp, email, address, hours, About text, founder name, Instagram/Facebook links, Google review link, cancellation policy
- **Headline numbers** ("500+ happy travellers", "15 years"): hidden until set. Only enter figures the business can stand behind
- **Tours:** price, photos, best time, group size, places covered, day-by-day plan, inclusions/exclusions, show or hide
- **Cars:** seats, bags, price per day, features, photo, show or hide
- **Destinations:** description, places, photo
- **Staff:** add or remove staff logins
